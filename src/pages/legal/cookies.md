This notice explains how www.greensquare.ai, run by GreenSquare AI, uses cookies and similar technologies. Read it alongside the [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/).

## What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website stores on your device. It lets the site work properly and remember basic technical details between pages.

## Which cookies we use

We keep cookies to a minimum. We do not run advertising cookies, and we do not use trackers that follow you across other websites.

- **Functional cookies.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, may set functional cookies so pages load and behave correctly.
- **Analytics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and see which sites link to us, and to count a small set of named site events: an email request attempted, accepted or failed; the PDF offered on the page; a PDF link selected; the start guide viewed; the starter text copied; and an example selected. It sets no cookies, stores no identifier on your device, and does not follow you to other websites. No event carries your email address or anything you type. We use it to understand which pages are useful, never to build a profile of you or to target advertising.
- **Local storage.** The site keeps one record in your browser's local storage, under the key `gs_acquisition_v3`. It is not a cookie. It holds the path of the page you first landed on and, where the link you followed carried campaign tags (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, `utm_content` or `gs_channel`), a shortened form of those tags, for your first visit and your latest tagged visit. A short label made from it is attached to the site events above. The [privacy policy](/legal/privacy/) describes it in full.

We do not set cookies to identify you personally. The only personal detail you give us is your email address, entered yourself on a form.

## Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser. Most browsers let you see what is stored, block cookies, or delete them. Clearing this site's data also removes the local storage record. Blocking functional cookies may stop some parts of the site behaving as intended. Nothing on the site requires a cookie or local storage to request or download the Frame Free PDF.

## Changes to this notice

We update this notice when our use of cookies changes. The date at the top shows the current version.
