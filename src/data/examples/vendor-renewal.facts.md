# Warehouse system replacement

## Opening message
Our warehouse management system contract runs out on 30 November and the vendor says the version we're on is losing support, so I need to pick between two replacement platforms by the end of October. Picking errors are costing us money and I want this sorted before the Christmas peak.

## Decision owner and due date
- Operations manager of a 45-person wholesale food distributor supplying cafes and restaurants in south-east Queensland.
- Wants a contract signed by 31 October 2026.

## Presented choice
- Platform A: $68,000 implementation, $2,900 a month, three-year term.
- Platform B: $41,000 implementation, $3,600 a month, three-year term.

## Trigger
- Current contract end date of 30 November 2026.
- A call and follow-up email from the current vendor's account manager saying the installed version "moves to limited support".

## Known facts
- The warehouse ships about 10,000 order lines a week.
- Picking error rate over the last quarter was 2.1%, about 210 wrong lines a week.
- Credit notes issued for picking errors last quarter totalled $14,200.
- Three-year cost: Platform A $172,400, Platform B $170,600.

## Constraints as the owner currently believes them
- The current system cannot be used after 30 November [not checked: nobody has read the renewal or termination clauses in the contract].
- Implementation must finish before the December peak [checked: peak dates come from last year's sales data].
- Budget for the year is $90,000 for systems projects [checked: approved by the managing director].
- Both new platforms will cut picking errors [not checked: based on vendor demonstrations only].

## Explicit unknowns
- What "limited support" means in practice.
- Whether either new platform integrates with the accounting system without custom work.

## Background the owner knows but would only mention if asked
- About 60% of picking errors happen on the night shift, which moved to mostly casual pickers in March 2026.
- The racking was reconfigured in February 2026 and some bin labels were not updated.
- Error rates were below 1% for the two years before March 2026, on the same software.
- The current vendor sent a quote to upgrade to their newer version in September. It has not been opened.

## Hidden framing issue (for the record only; the operator must never volunteer this)
The error rate rose when staffing and bin labelling changed, not when the software changed, so a new platform may not fix the problem. The 30 November deadline and the support end date come from a sales conversation and have not been checked against the contract.
