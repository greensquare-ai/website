# Senior engineer hire

## Opening message
We keep delivering drawing packages late and two clients have complained this quarter, so I'm deciding whether to hire another senior structural engineer. I'd like to advertise the role before the end of the month.

## Decision owner and due date
- Managing principal of an 18-person structural engineering consultancy in Adelaide.
- Wants to decide by 30 October 2026 so the role can be advertised.

## Presented choice
- Hire one senior structural engineer at about $145,000 a year plus superannuation.
- Or keep the current team and accept the late deliveries.

## Trigger
- Written complaints from two repeat clients in the September quarter about late packages.

## Known facts
- 41 of the 63 projects delivered in the last 12 months were late, about 65%.
- Average lateness was 9 working days.
- The team is 6 engineers, 5 drafters, 1 principal reviewer and 6 support and admin staff.
- Engineer billable utilisation averaged 78% last financial year.

## Constraints as the owner currently believes them
- A senior engineer would take about three months to recruit [not checked: based on the last hire, in 2023].
- The salary has to be at least $145,000 to attract anyone [not checked: no market data gathered].
- Every package must be signed off by a principal before issue [checked: required by the professional indemnity insurer for this practice].
- Only the managing principal can do that sign-off [not checked: assumed; nobody has asked the insurer whether another registered engineer could].

## Explicit unknowns
- How many hours a week the new hire would actually free up.
- Whether clients would pay more for faster turnaround.

## Background the owner knows but would only mention if asked
- The project system shows packages sitting in "awaiting principal review" for an average of 6 working days.
- The managing principal reviews every package personally and also runs business development.
- Drafters regularly report waiting for markups before they can continue.
- About 40% of the late projects had client-requested design changes after the fee was agreed. Most of those changes were absorbed without a variation.
- Two engineers have said privately they have spare time some weeks.

## Hidden framing issue (for the record only; the operator must never volunteer this)
Engineering capacity may not be the bottleneck. Work waits about 6 days for a single reviewer, and unpriced scope changes cause rework, so a new engineer could add output into the same queue without fixing delivery times.
