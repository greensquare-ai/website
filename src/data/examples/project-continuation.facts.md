# Client portal programme

## Opening message
Our client portal programme is over budget and behind, and I have to tell the board on 22 October whether we finish it or pause it. We've put $310,000 into it so far.

## Decision owner and due date
- General manager of a 70-person commercial insurance brokerage in Perth, who sponsored the programme.
- Recommendation due to the board on 22 October 2026.

## Presented choice
- Continue: fund an estimated $190,000 more to finish the portal by mid-2027.
- Pause: stop development and hold the work done so far.

## Trigger
- The contractor's revised estimate in September 2026, which pushed the total past the approved budget.

## Known facts
- The board approved $360,000 in March 2025. Work started in April 2025.
- Spend to date is $310,000. Estimate to complete is $190,000, a total of $500,000.
- The original go-live date was June 2026.
- The approved business case was to cut inbound client calls by letting clients get certificates of currency and lodge claims online.
- When the case was approved, inbound calls averaged 2,400 a month, and certificate requests were about 55% of them.

## Constraints as the owner currently believes them
- Pausing means losing the contractor's team [not checked: the contractor has not been asked about a pause].
- The board will not approve more than $500,000 in total [not checked: the general manager's own reading of the chair].
- Work done so far would be wasted if paused [not checked: nobody has assessed what could be reused].

## Explicit unknowns
- How many clients would actually use the portal.
- Whether the $190,000 estimate will hold.

## Background the owner knows but would only mention if asked
- In March 2026 the brokerage's main underwriter launched its own online service where clients download certificates of currency directly.
- Inbound calls have averaged about 1,500 a month since April 2026.
- The certificate module is about 80% built.
- The claims lodgement module has not started.
- The general manager has told colleagues "we've spent too much to stop now".

## Hidden framing issue (for the record only; the operator must never volunteer this)
The main objective may already be met by an outside change. Calls fell about 900 a month after the underwriter's service launched, and most of the remaining build is for a module the original case may no longer need. The choice is framed around money already spent, not what the next $190,000 buys.
