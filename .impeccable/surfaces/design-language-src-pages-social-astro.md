# Social system surface brief

- **Scope and mode:** `/social` is a Read + Operate application inside the GreenSquare design-language shell.
- **Audience:** GreenSquare operators and collaborators producing institutional social content for decision-work practitioners.
- **Job:** Choose a channel and content job, preserve the evidence chain, review the approved post direction and format standard, pass QA, and export a native derivative.
- **Primary action:** Enter the channel chooser; the public conversion action is the free Decision Frame.
- **Proof:** Restrained evidence notation, a live decision-ledger specimen, two user-approved post mockups, exact format standards, official channel guidance, and explicit export blockers.
- **Constraints:** Faceless; no spoken narration, AI avatars, or synthetic voice; essential meaning independent of sound, motion, and colour; GitHub updates must be substantive.
- **Direction:** A conservative, MBB-aligned extension of the existing black/forest/paper system: orthogonal fields, flat working records, tonal forest geometry, strict axes, quiet metadata, and no decorative sheet rotation or novelty iconography.
- **Memorable moment:** The first viewport turns a governing question into a flat, inspectable decision record; the next section makes the approved square post references visible at full reading size.
- **Unresolved:** Canva production is deliberately on hold until this amended social direction is approved. The standalone v1 deployment can be redirected or retired after the canonical `/social` production deployment is confirmed. External posts require account access and confirmation at posting time.
