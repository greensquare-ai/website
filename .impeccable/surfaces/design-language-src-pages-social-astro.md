# Social system surface brief

- **Scope and mode:** `/social` is a Read + Operate application inside the GreenSquare design-language shell.
- **Audience:** GreenSquare operators and collaborators producing institutional social content for decision-work practitioners.
- **Job:** Choose a channel and content job, preserve the evidence chain, open the correct Canva master, pass QA, and export a native derivative.
- **Primary action:** Enter the channel chooser; the public conversion action is the free Decision Frame.
- **Proof:** Canonical evidence notation, a live decision-ledger specimen, exact-size Canva masters, official channel guidance, and explicit export blockers.
- **Constraints:** Faceless; no spoken narration, AI avatars, or synthetic voice; essential meaning independent of sound, motion, and colour; GitHub updates must be substantive.
- **Direction:** A code-led extension of the existing black/forest/paper system, organised as an operating ledger rather than a template gallery.
- **Memorable moment:** The first viewport turns a governing question into confirmed evidence, assumption, material risk, recommendation, owner, and reversal.
- **Unresolved:** Canva Brand Kit inspection/configuration needs a refreshed `brandkit:read` connector scope. The standalone v1 deployment can be redirected or retired after the canonical `/social` production deployment is confirmed. External posts require account access and confirmation at posting time.
