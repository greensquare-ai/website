# GreenSquare Commercial Architecture

Status: working hypothesis, not yet a locked pricing model

## Commercial objective

Prove that GreenSquare can generate revenue from a scalable product experience without requiring the founder to deliver each engagement personally.

The commercial system should serve busy, potentially sceptical professionals who already use AI to some degree and need a clear reason to use GreenSquare rather than normal prompting.

## Product ladder

### Free: Decision Frame

Role:

- acquisition
- demonstration of the GreenSquare method
- email capture
- proof of value
- product-learning surface

The free product must be genuinely useful. Do not deliberately cripple it to manufacture an upgrade.

### Paid 1: Decision / Decision Brief

Role:

- deeper recommendation system
- more complete decision-grade output
- additional repeatability, artefact or specialist capability as validated by users

Current commercial boundary must be revalidated after Decision Frame V2 testing.

### Paid 2: Strategy Execution / Execution Blueprint

Role:

- translate decisions into executable programs
- owners, workstreams, milestones, dependencies, governance, capability and risk

### Paid 3: Value Realisation System

Role:

- track whether execution produces intended value
- monitor KPIs, benefits, assumptions and course-correction triggers

## Underlying progression

Judgement -> Decision -> Execution -> Realised value.

## Unresolved commercial decisions

Before locking the model, validate:

- strongest natural free-to-paid boundary
- one-off purchase versus subscription/recurring relationship
- whether persistence/history is a paid value driver
- specialised decision modules versus broad engine
- pricing and willingness to pay
- what users repeatedly return for
- whether team/enterprise use emerges from individual adoption

## Commercial principle

Do not choose monetisation by withholding arbitrary pieces of the methodology. Make the free front door excellent, observe where users seek additional depth or continuity, then monetise the next valuable job.
