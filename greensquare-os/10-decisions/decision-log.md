# GreenSquare Decision Log

## 21 August 2026

### V4 is canonical

Decision: V4 is the sole governing GreenSquare brand/design system. V1-V3 are historical drafts.

Implication: future agents and contributors must not resolve current design decisions from V1-V3.

### Hierarchy is the governing visual principle

Decision: nothing should overpower the idea being communicated. Hero content leads; brand marks remain restrained.

### GreenSquare is a judgement system

Decision: the core asset is the machine-readable judgement system, not any single PDF, website or prompt.

### Six stages are states, not questions

Decision: Decision Frame V2 will use state-based progression. The model seeks sufficient understanding for the next stage rather than mechanically asking six fixed questions.

### GreenSquare shapes process, not the answer

Decision: GreenSquare provides structure, interrogation, evidence discipline, option logic and execution thinking. It does not define a universal correct decision.

### Decision scorecard established early

Decision: the user and AI define contextual success criteria near the beginning and reuse them when comparing options and validating the recommendation.

### Rigour scales with stakes

Decision: interrogation and evidence requirements scale with consequence, scrutiny and reversibility.

### Hypotheses are provisional

Decision: goal-led and evidence-led hypothesis formation are both valid. Material new information should change the work; sunk analytical effort has no standing.

### Relevance determines option count

Decision: GreenSquare will not force a three-option structure. Relevant options may be sequenced NOW / NEXT / LATER / MONITOR.

### Do nothing is a legitimate managed option

Decision: maintaining the current position can be recommended, but must include monitoring, thresholds and triggers for reopening the decision.

### Proceed, do not pretend

Decision: users may proceed with known gaps, but gaps remain visible in confidence, risk, monitoring and review triggers.

### Decision threshold

Decision: proceed when evidence and hypothesis align sufficiently that further delay is less valuable than learning through action.

### Execution fitness is part of decision quality

Decision: recommendations should be tested against real owners, capabilities, capacity, dependencies, timing, governance and operating constraints as relevant.

### Adoption starts before final recommendation

Decision: progressive synthesis, stakeholder consultation and showing progress are part of effective decision work. Objective recommendation remains distinct from adoption strategy.

### Free product should be genuinely useful

Decision: do not cripple the Decision Frame to manufacture paid conversion. Validate the natural paid boundary after testing a strong free experience.

### Content model

Decision: one canonical idea should be transformed by channel. Human involvement should focus on rapid QA and teaching the system recurring editorial preferences rather than writing every asset manually.
