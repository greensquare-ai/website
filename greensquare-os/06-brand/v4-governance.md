# GreenSquare V4 Governance

## Authority

V4 is the sole governing GreenSquare brand and design system.

V1, V2 and V3 are historical drafts. They must not be used to resolve current design decisions or generate new assets.

Where older repository documentation conflicts with V4, V4 wins and the conflicting documentation should be marked historical or corrected.

## Governing visual principle

Nothing should overpower the idea being communicated.

The content is the attraction. Branding is the system around it.

Therefore:

- hero message carries primary hierarchy
- supporting copy remains subordinate
- logo and iconography remain restrained
- brand marks generally occupy secondary positions such as corners rather than competing with the message
- colour follows V4
- typography and hierarchy follow V4
- visuals should communicate systems, structure and real decision work rather than generic AI imagery

## Launch tolerance

Minor spacing, negative-space and micro-layout refinements are polish. They should not delay launch when the V4 hierarchy, typography, palette and governing principles are correctly applied.

## Writing governance

GreenSquare writing should be human, executive, precise and editorial where appropriate.

Requirements:

- Australian English
- no em dashes
- no emojis in brand/editorial material
- no generic AI hype
- no bloated consultancy language
- no artificial cleverness
- no fake vulnerability
- no engagement bait
- lead with the implication
- use concrete nouns
- make useful distinctions
- earn certainty

## Agent rule

Agents may vary layout and expression within V4, but may not invent a parallel visual language. If a required pattern does not exist, flag the gap before creating a new governing convention.
