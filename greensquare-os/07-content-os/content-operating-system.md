# GreenSquare Content Operating System

## Objective

Turn GreenSquare intellectual source material into consistent, useful, V4-compliant content that can be adapted across channels with minimal manual writing.

The launch goal is to start publishing, learn from the market and drive qualified users towards the free Decision Frame.

## Launch channels

- Instagram
- TikTok
- X
- LinkedIn, introduced at launch or shortly thereafter

## Core transformation model

One canonical idea -> channel-specific transformations.

Do not create four unrelated ideas merely because four channels exist.

Channel roles:

- X: sharpen
- LinkedIn: explain
- Instagram: make memorable
- TikTok/Reels: demonstrate

## Content territories

1. Frame the real decision.
2. Widen the option set.
3. Find the critical assumption.
4. Separate evidence from belief.
5. Compare what matters.
6. Move to action.

## Workflow

1. Source: draw from Judgment OS, products, evidence, market observations and founder/operator insights.
2. Select: identify one useful decision insight.
3. Draft: generate the canonical argument in GreenSquare voice.
4. Transform: adapt to each selected channel's native format.
5. Design: apply V4 components and hierarchy.
6. QA: human reviews meaning, tone and visual fit.
7. Learn: capture the reason for edits as reusable rules.
8. Publish: ship approved content.
9. Measure: observe resonance, qualified traffic and conversion.
10. Feed back: use evidence to improve topic selection, writing and creative structure.

## Human QA model

The human should not need to write every asset from scratch.

Target workflow:

Agent generates finished draft -> human makes quick edits -> human explains material dislikes/changes -> system captures the rule -> future generations improve.

The long-term objective is a machine-readable `Karim QA` layer that encodes repeated editorial judgements.

## Writing requirements

Content must feel human, executive, tidy and editorial where appropriate.

Avoid:

- em dashes
- emojis
- generic AI language
- filler
- engagement bait
- overexplaining
- consultancy jargon without a useful distinction

## Launch CTA

Launch-period content should primarily drive interested users towards the free Decision Frame. Avoid competing calls to action until there is evidence that a more complex funnel is required.

## Minimum viable launch system

A launch-ready system can:

- select a source idea
- produce one canonical argument
- generate Instagram carousel structure
- generate TikTok carousel/visual structure
- generate X posts or threads
- generate LinkedIn adaptation
- apply V4 design logic
- present outputs for fast human approval
- capture QA feedback
- store approved content and publication status

Automated publishing is useful but is not required to prove the content system. Reliable generation and fast approval come first.
