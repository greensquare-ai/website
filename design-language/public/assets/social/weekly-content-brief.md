# GreenSquare weekly source brief

Complete this brief before producing any channel derivative. One brief equals one weekly source idea.

## Required fields

**slug**
Lowercase, hyphenated identifier used in filenames and `utm_content`.

**governing_question**
The consequential decision question the week will help the audience answer.

**audience_trigger**
The buying situation, pressure, or failure mode that makes this useful now.

**claim**
One bounded statement the evidence can support.

**evidence**
Facts, observations, product outputs, or sourced data. Label each material item as Confirmed, Assumption, Uncertainty, or Material risk.

**interpretation**
What GreenSquare believes the evidence means. Do not present this as a confirmed fact.

**assumptions**
The beliefs carrying the interpretation or recommendation, plus the fastest credible test for each.

**material_risk**
The evidenced or plausible downside that could alter the recommendation.

**recommendation**
The call, written as an action with a named owner.

**reversal_condition**
The observable threshold, event, or date that requires the recommendation to be revisited.

**product_demo_step**
The truthful GreenSquare interface or document step that demonstrates the method. Label simulated screens or data.

**source_urls**
Direct URLs to primary or authoritative sources. Missing source URLs block export.

**cta**
Default: Download the free Decision Frame.

**channel_notes**
Native hook, pacing, interaction, moderation, safe-zone, export, and caption notes for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, and GitHub.

## Final release statement

- [ ] The claim is supported by the listed evidence.
- [ ] Evidence and interpretation are visibly distinct.
- [ ] Material assumptions and risk are written, not implied.
- [ ] The recommendation has an owner and reversal condition.
- [ ] The derivative is useful in its channel without relying on the link.
