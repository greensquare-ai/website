# GreenSquare four-week social pilot

## Week 1 — Reframe the buying situation

**Governing question:** What decision are we actually making when we compare AI tools?
**Source idea:** The decision is not “which AI tool?” It is which workflow, owner, risk boundary, and review standard should change.
**Lead format:** LinkedIn 4:5 PDF carousel.
**Product proof:** Complete Question 1 of the Decision Frame.
**Primary CTA:** Download the Decision Frame.

## Week 2 — Separate evidence from interpretation

**Governing question:** Which part of an AI recommendation is known, and which part is judgement?
**Source idea:** Evidence does not recommend itself. The interpretation must stay visible.
**Lead format:** Instagram carousel plus kinetic Reel.
**Product proof:** Show Confirmed, Assumption, and Uncertainty labels on one decision.
**Primary CTA:** Download the Decision Frame.

## Week 3 — State what changes the call

**Governing question:** What would have to become true for this recommendation to reverse?
**Source idea:** A recommendation without a reversal condition is harder to govern.
**Lead format:** X reversal card plus concise thread.
**Product proof:** Show the reversal-condition field being completed.
**Primary CTA:** Download the Decision Frame.

## Week 4 — Demonstrate the complete arc

**Governing question:** How does a weak brief become a defensible call?
**Source idea:** Show the transition from a narrow prompt to an owned recommendation with evidence, risk, and reversal.
**Lead format:** TikTok and Instagram text-and-sound walkthrough; GitHub example if a substantive artefact is ready.
**Product proof:** A truthful interface recording or labelled simulation.
**Primary CTA:** Download the Decision Frame.

## Measurement

1. Decision Frame downloads and landing-page conversion.
2. Saves, completion, qualified comments, and profile or site visits on primary channels.
3. Helpful discussion quality and community-rule compliance on Reddit.
4. Repository traffic, stars, clones, or meaningful product actions on GitHub.

After Week 4, retain the two strongest buying situations and formats. Revise cadence without changing canonical brand invariants.
