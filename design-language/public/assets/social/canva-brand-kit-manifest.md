# GreenSquare Canva Brand Kit manifest

## Logos

Upload and name the supplied assets exactly:

- `GS — Lockup — Primary black`
- `GS — Lockup — Forest`
- `GS — Lockup — Light`
- `GS — Lockup — Mono ink`
- `GS — Mark — White`
- `GS — Mark — Forest`

The full lockup introduces GreenSquare on every standalone or introductory post. The mark alone is for already-labelled or repeated contexts. The material leaf is an illustration derived from the mark; it never replaces the lockup.

## Brand colours

### Core and supporting

- Black — `#10110F`
- Forest — `#133F26`
- Deep — `#0B2313`
- Ink — `#12140F`
- White — `#FFFFFF`
- Ground — `#F5F5F5`
- Line — `#DEDAD2`
- Muted — `#63645C`

### Social application

- Paper — `#FBF8F0`
- Mineral — `#C7CBC0`

Cream and Chalk are retired.

### Evidence states

- Confirmed — `#24553B`
- Assumption — `#745B2D`
- Uncertainty — `#686B65`
- Material risk — `#77372F`

Evidence colour never works alone. Keep the written state label whenever the state changes interpretation.

## Type

- Decisive display — Space Grotesk Semibold
- Functional content — IBM Plex Sans Regular, Medium, Semibold, or Bold
- Literal code only — a legible monospaced font available in the Canva account

Decorative terminal styling is prohibited.

## Imagery

Use truthful product UI, real documents, material photography, sourced data, and interface demonstrations. Label simulated screens or data. Do not use presenters, faces, personal-brand portraits, AI avatars, synthetic voice, or decorative stock scenes.

## Master naming

`GS — Social — <canvas> — <job> — MASTER`

Working copies use:

`<YYYY-MM-DD> — <weekly-slug> — <channel> — <format> — v<revision>`

## Lock in every master

- Logo clear space and approved variant
- Evidence marker geometry and colours
- Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Sans roles
- Source, owner, and reversal components
- Safe-zone overlays and editor labels
- Alt-text prompt and export notes

## Keep editable

- Governing question and audience trigger
- Claim, evidence, and interpretation
- Assumptions and material risk
- Recommendation, owner, and reversal condition
- Product demonstration and simulation label
- Source URL, CTA, UTM content, captions, and alt text
