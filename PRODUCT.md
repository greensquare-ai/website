# GreenSquare AI Product Context

## Platform

GreenSquare AI is a decision-intelligence system and public knowledge platform. Its owned surfaces include the GreenSquare marketing site, the Decision Frame lead resource, the GreenSquare design-language site, product demonstrations, and public repositories. The design-language site is the canonical record for brand applications, including social publishing.

## Users

Primary users are AI-forward practitioners responsible for consequential organisational decisions: chiefs of staff, strategy and operations leaders, transformation and programme leads, product-operations practitioners, and AI-adoption leaders. Initial market emphasis is the United States, Australia, and the United Kingdom, followed by other English-speaking markets with high AI adoption.

Users need to turn incomplete evidence, competing interpretations, assumptions, constraints, and downside risk into a recommendation that can be inspected, challenged, owned, and reversed when conditions change.

## Product Purpose

GreenSquare helps teams make consequential decisions defensible. It does this by making the decision frame, evidence state, interpretation, assumptions, material risk, recommendation, ownership, and reversal conditions explicit.

The free Decision Frame is the primary public entry point. It helps a user frame the choice before AI or analysis begins and leads into the fuller GreenSquare decision process.

## Positioning

GreenSquare is the decision-intelligence system for making consequential decisions defensible. It is not a generic AI assistant, content studio, project-management tool, or thought-leadership personality. Its authority comes from transparent evidence, explicit judgement, inspectable reasoning, and product proof.

## Operating Context

GreenSquare is an institutional, faceless brand. Public communication is designed for professional contexts where the audience may need to defend a recommendation to executives, boards, clients, regulators, or cross-functional stakeholders.

Social publishing uses one strong source idea each week and adapts it natively for LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, X, Reddit, and GitHub. LinkedIn, Instagram, and TikTok are primary reach and authority channels. X, Reddit, and GitHub are secondary proof, discussion, and product-action channels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The product can be demonstrated through truthful interface recordings, real decision artefacts, sourced public data, and real documents.
- Simulated product screens or demonstration data must be labelled as simulated.
- Public evidence must retain source URLs and a clear distinction between evidence and interpretation.
- Every consequential recommendation must name its owner and reversal condition.
- Social video uses text, motion, and commercially licensed sound; essential information cannot depend on sound.
- No presenters, faces, personal-brand posting, spoken narration, AI avatars, or synthetic voice are used.
- GitHub activity must be substantive; cadence never justifies manufacturing a repository update.
- External publishing and account changes require account access and confirmation at posting time.
- The initial public launch is English-only.

## Brand Commitments

- GreenSquare makes evidence, interpretation, assumptions, uncertainty, and material risk visibly distinct.
- The supplied GreenSquare AI lockup introduces the organisation; the mark alone is reserved for already-recognised or explicitly labelled contexts.
- Public communication is calm, precise, useful, and direct. It avoids hype, false certainty, performance claims without evidence, and decorative technical theatre.
- The decision arc remains consistent across formats: frame the decision, inspect evidence, expose assumptions and risk, make the call, assign ownership, and state the reversal condition.
- Accessibility is part of credibility: meaning cannot rely on colour, sound, animation, or pointer interaction alone.

## Evidence on Hand

- A live GreenSquare marketing site and Decision Frame capture flow.
- A live design-language site with established identity, typography, decision notation, governance, and accessibility patterns.
- A prior social-system prototype that demonstrates content templates but is not the canonical system.
- Current official production guidance for Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Reddit, and GitHub.
- Product interface and real documents that can be demonstrated truthfully, subject to confidentiality and source review.

## Product Principles

1. Frame before solving.
2. Separate evidence from interpretation.
3. Keep assumptions and uncertainty visible.
4. Name the material downside.
5. Make every recommendation ownable and reversible.
6. Demonstrate the product instead of describing it abstractly.
7. Adapt the format by channel without changing the underlying judgement.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

GreenSquare targets WCAG 2.2 AA for owned web surfaces. Controls must be keyboard operable, visibly focused, and at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels where applicable. Pages require semantic landmarks, descriptive titles, useful alternative text, no horizontal overflow at supported widths, and reduced-motion fallbacks. Evidence states must be written in full whenever they change interpretation; status icons and colour alone are insufficient. Captions or visible text must carry all essential video meaning. Examples should avoid assuming a single national, organisational, or cultural context.
